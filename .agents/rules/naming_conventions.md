# Terminology & Naming Conventions

Never abbreviate "Kommunikationszentrum" as "KZ". In German and historical context, "KZ" refers to concentration camps (*Konzentrationslager*) and is inappropriate and offensive.

Always write out "Kommunikationszentrum" in full, or use neutral, respectful abbreviations/synonyms such as:
- "Kommunikation"
- "KomZentr"
- "the platform" / "the server" / "the system"
